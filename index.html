About

I grew up in Gurugram in a family with no connection to film or photography. My first real lessons in seeing came from studio photography under Nitin Rai. My first real lesson in making something came from a school series called The Classroom, which I co-directed, shot and edited with friends.

I trained in cinematography and direction at Whistling Woods International in Mumbai. Alongside and after my degree I shot short and independent films, worked with the direction team on a commercial campaign, and now work as a camera assistant.

Over time the questions that kept me up changed. Less about where the light sits, more about how a film gets made at all: the schedule, the money, the people. That is where I am heading, toward producing, and one day a small studio of my own in India.
